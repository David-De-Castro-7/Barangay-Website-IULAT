Report an Urgent Community Issue

You can submit an urgent community concern without creating an iUlat account. Provide the location and details of the incident so authorized barangay personnel can review it.

⚠️ For Life-Threatening Emergencies

iUlat is intended for community reporting and barangay concerns. If there is an immediate threat to life, serious injury, fire, or another emergency requiring immediate response, please call 911.
