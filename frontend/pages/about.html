ABOUT iULAT

Connecting the Barangay
with Its Residents.

iUlat is a digital barangay community information platform created to make official information easier to access, understand, and share.

WHAT IS iULAT?

A better way to stay informed about your barangay.

iUlat is a web-based barangay community information platform designed to provide residents with timely, accessible, and reliable information.

Instead of relying on scattered social media posts, physical notices, or word-of-mouth communication, iUlat provides one centralized platform where residents can find official announcements, community updates, important notices, and other barangay information.

The platform is designed to strengthen communication between barangay officials and the community while making important information easier for residents to find.
