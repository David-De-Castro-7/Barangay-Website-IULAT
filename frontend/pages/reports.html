COMMUNITY REPORTING

Report a Community Concern

Help your barangay identify and respond to community concerns through the iUlat reporting system.

HOW IT WORKS

Your Report Can Help Improve the Community

Residents can use iUlat to submit concerns directly to authorized barangay officials. Reports can be reviewed, tracked, and updated through the resident dashboard.

R

Road & Infrastructure

Report damaged roads, potholes, broken sidewalks, drainage problems, and other infrastructure concerns.

S

Sanitation & Garbage

Report garbage accumulation, sanitation issues, and other environmental concerns.

L

Streetlights & Utilities

Report damaged streetlights and other public utility concerns within the barangay.

P

Public Safety

Report community safety concerns that require attention from barangay officials.

C

Community Concerns

Submit concerns involving community activities, public spaces, or barangay facilities.

O

Other Concerns

Have another barangay-related concern? You may submit it through the reporting system.

SIMPLE PROCESS

How Reporting Works

01

Sign In

Sign in to your resident account to access the reporting system.

02

Submit Your Report

Provide the details of the concern, including its location and description.

03

Barangay Review

Authorized officials review and evaluate the submitted report.

04

Track the Status

Follow the progress of your report through your resident dashboard.
