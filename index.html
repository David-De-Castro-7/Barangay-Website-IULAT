OUR PURPOSE

Vision and Mission

iUlat supports clearer, more accessible, and more reliable communication between the barangay government and the residents it serves.

VISION

A better-informed and connected barangay community.

Residents can easily access official information and stay aware of important barangay activities, advisories, programs, and community concerns.

MISSION

Make official barangay information easier to find and understand.

iUlat provides one accessible channel for verified announcements, important notices, emergency information, and community updates.
